* Sub (-)
* Mul (*)
* Div (/)
* Mod (%)
* And (&)
* Or (|)
* Xor (^)
* Min
* Max
* Heaviside
* Abs
* Not
* sqrt
* round
* floor
* ceiling
* LessThan (<=)
* LessThanStrict (<)

* GreaterThanStrict (>)
* Equality (==)
* Unequality (!=)
* powi (expontentiation to an integer power)
* powi_mod (modular expontentiation to an integer power)
